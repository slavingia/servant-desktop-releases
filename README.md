# Servant for Mac

Download installers and setup instructions at [servant-desktop.web.app](https://servant-desktop.web.app).

Servant brings your channels, direct messages, and agent to your Mac. It requires macOS 13 or later.

1. Download the Apple silicon or Intel installer and drag Servant to Applications.
2. Update the Servant app on your phone to build 25 or later through your existing testing access.
3. On your phone, open **You → Link a computer**, scan the Mac’s QR code, compare the numbers, and approve.

The Mac uses your existing account with a separate device credential. Your phone stays signed in.

## Preview status

The initial release has ad-hoc signatures and is **not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized**. macOS Gatekeeper may block it. See [Apple’s guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) for opening an app from an unidentified developer; managed Macs may require IT approval.

Checksums accompany each release. This repository contains download artifacts and installation information.
